**ControllerRemap 0.3.1**

Custom controller layouts for Farever, including shoulder/trigger combos and weapon/class mode holds.
Save and share profiles, inspect bindings with the clickable controller diagram, and resolve exact conflicts by moving or swapping buttons.
Different input contexts, holds and modes stay separate.

Standalone HLX mod; SFimgui helper included. Press **F10**.
